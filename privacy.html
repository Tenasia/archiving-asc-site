Privacy Policy

Archiving ASC · Last updated October 1, 2026

Archiving ASC ("the app") is a self-hosted tool. The person who deploys it ("the operator") runs it on their own server for their own use. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Google user data the app accesses

The app requests a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can:

  • create an archive folder, its subfolders and a log spreadsheet in the operator's Google Drive;
  • upload images into those folders and move them between those folders;
  • append and update rows in that log spreadsheet.

The app can't see, read or change any other file in the operator's Google Drive.

Other data the app processes

  • WhatsApp messages: for group chats the operator has chosen, the app receives images together with their caption, sender name, sender phone number, group ID and timestamp. Text-only messages are ignored.
  • Image classification: images without a category tag may be sent to Google's Gemini API to suggest a folder. Only the image and its caption are sent.

Where data is stored

  • Images and the log spreadsheet are stored in the operator's own Google Drive.
  • The OAuth refresh token, image metadata and small image thumbnails are stored on the operator's own server.

Sharing

The app doesn't sell, rent or share data with anyone. It doesn't use data for advertising. Apart from the operator's own Google Drive and Sheets, data is sent only to the Gemini API, for classification. No public sharing links are created for archived files.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Archived images stay in the operator's Google Drive until the operator deletes them. To stop all access, the operator can revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/connections. Data stored on the server is deleted when the operator removes the deployment and its volumes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
